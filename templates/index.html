About Enerlytics India
Data-driven exploration of India's energy patterns

Enerlytics India is a data analytics dashboard designed to explore state-wise energy patterns using historical data.It uses data visualization and statistical analysis to identify trends, compare states, study distributions, examine correlations, and detect unusual patterns in energy values.
